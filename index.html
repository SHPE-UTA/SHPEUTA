About the chapter

A chapter that grows engineers and leaders

SHPE describes its mission as empowering the Hispanic community to realize its fullest potential and impact the world through STEM awareness, access, support, and development. SHPE chapter resources also describe an Executive Board model led by officers, while allowing chapters to add additional leadership roles that fit local needs.
